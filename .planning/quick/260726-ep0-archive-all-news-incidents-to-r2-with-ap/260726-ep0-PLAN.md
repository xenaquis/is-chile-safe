<objective>
Publish a citable research dataset of every classified news incident (current + all monthly archives) to the user's Cloudflare R2 bucket, refreshed daily, with a Spanish APA-7 citation attached to each record — AND archive the full article text of every incident (append-only) for future sentiment/text analysis, alongside a continuously-updated corpus-state + URL ledger that tracks fetch accounting.

Output: `pipeline/archive_r2.py`, `pipeline/news/fulltext.py`, a daily `.github/workflows/r2-archive.yml` cron, `boto3` + `trafilatura` in requirements, and `pipeline/tests/test_archive_r2.py`.
