<objective>
Fix the post-Granite-migration bug where sexual crimes (violacion, abuso sexual, estupro,
grooming, connotacion sexual) are mis-classified as family "vida" because the news classifier
enum has no sexual-crimes option. Introduce a NEWS-ONLY family "sexuales" (the CEAD quantitative
7-family contract stays untouched), repair the already-corrupted data locally and in the R2
archive with a deterministic keyword sweep, and wire the site to redirect Spanish-preferring
browsers from EN pages to their ES equivalent.

Purpose: Sexual crimes are a distinct, high-salience category; bucketing them under generic
"Life Crimes" is editorially wrong and inflates the vida count (628 of 1192). Chilean visitors
should land in Spanish by default.

Output: Classifier + schema + frontend labels for "sexuales"; a repaired current.json + R2
archive; a browser-language redirect in BaseLayout.
</objective>
