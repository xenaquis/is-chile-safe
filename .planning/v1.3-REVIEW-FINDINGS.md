# Chile Safety Map — v1.3 Review Findings

**Date:** 2026-06-18
**Synthesizer:** Opus (7-dimension swarm review + adversarial verdicts)

## Executive Summary

The v1.3 review surfaced **24 actionable findings** after dedupe and dropping verified-clean items (19 info/already-fixed items are listed in the appendix). Severity breakdown of survivors: **0 blocker, 4 high, 12 medium/should, 8 low/defer**. The four HIGH findings cluster into two headline issues. **(1) Methodology misrepresents its own math:** both methodology pages (EN/ES) claim a *population-weighted* national mean, but the code computes an *unweighted* mean — and the is-chile-safe FAQ already correctly says "unweighted" (MC-01, MC-07; adversarially confirmed against `data.ts`). This is a direct credibility/trust defect on the site's core claim. **(2) Bilingual integrity gaps:** the ES word "comuna" is consistently mis-spelled as the French/English "commune" across 4–7 ES pages including meta descriptions and JSON-LD (BP-02), and `/es/delitos-por-region/` is an ES-only orphan whose lang switcher loops to itself and emits no EN hreflang (BP-01). **Source attribution** has two HIGH gaps: ENUSC is cited as evidence for cifra-negra claims but absent from SOURCES.md (SC-03), and the INE population denominator — backing every per-100k rate — has only a one-line footnote (SC-01, downgraded to medium by skeptic). Data correctness is solid: the verify harness passes 8/2/0 and the pipeline suite passes 158/158; all P17 anomalies hold. No forbidden absolute safe/dangerous verdicts were found. **No release-blocking defects** — v1.3 is a polish/hardening milestone, not a rescue.
