### WARNING-1 — CI-06 caveat is not applied to every index-displaying page

The composite index is displayed on A-vs-B pages (`compare/[pair].astro:149,158`; `es/comparar/[pair].astro`) and in the comparator island headline, but `ci_caveat_text` — the string carrying the SII firm-domicile clause and the 2024 vintage — is rendered at exactly three call sites: `commune/[slug].astro:357`, `es/comuna/[slug].astro:360`, `ResultPanel.tsx:286`. A repo-wide grep for `ci_caveat_text` returns only those three plus the string definitions.

Mitigation already present: A-vs-B pages render `MethodologyCaveat` (`compare/[pair].astro:273`), the prose itself states the index is "weighted by population and economic-activity proxies" (`comparisonProse.ts:145`), and the island links "what is this?" to `/methodology/`. So this is an incompleteness against CI-06's literal wording, not an editorial-compliance failure — `forbidden-language` exits 0.

**Severity: WARNING.** Fix is one component insertion on two page templates plus the island.

### WARNING-2 — comparator_table.json is an orphan artifact

CI-10 required emitting `data/cead/comparator_table.json` "for Track 2". It is emitted (105,935 B), synced to `site/public/data/cead/`, and shipped to `site/dist/data/cead/comparator_table.json` (105,935 B). **No file under `site/src` references it** — a grep for comparator_table / comparator-table / comparatorTable across `site/src` returns zero matches. Phase 21 instead built on `loadIndex()` (`compare/index.astro:16`) plus per-commune lazy fetch (`ComparatorIsland.tsx:170`), which is the better design for payload size.

Consequence: ~106 KB of dead weight in every deploy, and a summary claim that is literally true but implies a consumer that does not exist. Not a broken flow — the comparator works without it.

**Severity: WARNING.** Either delete the artifact and its public sync, or record it explicitly as an analysis-only export.

### WARNING-3 — rank-direction gloss is inconsistent across index surfaces (Phase 31 spillover)

Phase 31 corrected the inverted national_rank claim on `methodology.astro`, `es/metodologia.astro` and `safest-cities-in-chile.astro`, and added a DOCS-01 build guard covering **only those three files**.

- Correctly glossed: `i18n.ts:259` rank_sublabel "1 = most reported incidence" and `:493` its ES twin; A-vs-B prose `comparisonProse.ts:198` "rank 1 = highest reported incidence", `:287` ES, `:209` and `:299` regional. Confirmed in built output — the gloss is present in `dist/compare/10101-vs-10202/index.html`, `dist/es/comparar/10101-vs-10202/index.html` and `dist/commune/santiago/index.html`.
- **Not glossed:** the composite-index rank line. `i18n.ts:415-416` (`ci_rank_national`, `ci_rank_regional`) render as a bare "#N of 346 nationally" at `commune/[slug].astro:353-355` and its ES twin, and in the same bare form inside `ComparatorIsland.tsx`. On commune pages the other rank card immediately above carries the gloss, so a reader there is not misled; a reader inside the comparator island has no gloss anywhere on screen.

**Severity: WARNING.** Add the directional suffix to `ci_rank_national` in both locales, and widen the DOCS-01 guard beyond its three-file allowlist so this class of regression is caught generally.
