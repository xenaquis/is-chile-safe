<objective>
Build a model-parameterized eval runner (V-08) and run the NREC-01 A/B over the 4 G-02 candidates, plus the DeepSeek-direct backup as an informational row that doubles as the NREC-04 funded/reachable smoke test. The winner is picked mechanically under G-02.

Purpose: the replacement for the delisted `ibm-granite/granite-4.1-8b` is chosen by pre-declared metrics, never by preference. The runner also measures the reasoning-disable flag (Research Pitfall 1 / Open Question 1), because 34-02 depends on that finding.

Output: `eval_classifier.py` and its tests, a minimal seam in `classifier.py`, and the committed `34-AB-RESULTS.json` / `.md`.

**Why a new file instead of extending `ab_score.py` (the smaller change here):** ROADMAP Phase 34 SC1 names `pipeline/experiments/eval_classifier.py --model` literally. `ab_score.py` is a historical Phase-16 harness with problems for this use:
- It imports the classifier through module globals, one provider per process (`ab_score.py:105-121`).
- Its `--provider` choices are `deepseek` and `minimax` only (`:274-278`).
- It conflates API errors and low-confidence rejections into `parse_failure` (`:155-162`, `:217`).
- It has no usage or reasoning capture and no spend meter.

Extending it would mean rewriting all of that and changing the meaning of the Phase-16 numbers already committed. `ab_score.py` stays untouched.

**Ordering vs 34-02:** this plan adds only the minimal seam: a pure refactor of `classifier.py` into `_request_completion(...)` + `_parse_content(...)`, with `classify()` behaviour unchanged. The runner injects client, model and extra_body per call, so no NEWS_MODEL env or constant edit is needed. 34-02 then builds config, retry, breaker and typed outcomes on the same seam. The two plans share `classifier.py`, so 34-02 is strictly after this one.
