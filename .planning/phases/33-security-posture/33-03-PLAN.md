Grep-exit-code discipline (F-117, binding correction to the research draft): every
evidence-gathering `grep` in this script must NOT be followed by `2>/dev/null || true`. Let
`set -euo pipefail` propagate a real grep error (rc >= 2: bad path, unreadable file, bad
regex) as a hard script failure. Only rc 0 (matches found) and rc 1 (no matches, clean) are
legitimate outcomes for the `while read` loop's input command; distinguish rc 1 (acceptable,
continue) from rc >= 2 (hard fail) explicitly rather than blanket-swallowing with `|| true`.
